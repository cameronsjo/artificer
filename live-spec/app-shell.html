app shell.

The responsive scaffold under every Artificer tool-SPA: an appbar across the top, a sidebar that collapses to the off-canvas drawer at --bp-tablet (resize narrow to see it), and a content track that is overflow-safe by construction — minmax(0,1fr), not bare 1fr. Tools fill; nothing here caps width.

Normal content

Direct children of .app-shell__content sit in the content column with a gutter on each side — the page body.

Full-bleed — reaches the pane edge

A .full-bleed child breaks the content gutter to span the whole pane (no 100vw, no negative margins — the same grid-bleed mechanism as the document .measure-grid). A scroll-snap filmstrip is the canonical use:

Overflow stress test

A <pre> with one very long unbroken line. With minmax(0,1fr) the content track shrinks to the viewport and the pre scrolls within itself; the page never scrolls sideways. (Under bare 1fr this exact element blew agentic-harnesses to ~2000px.)

$ kubectl get pods --all-namespaces -o wide --field-selector=status.phase=Running --sort-by=.metadata.creationTimestamp | grep -E 'ingress|gateway|mesh' | awk '{print $1, $2, $7}' | column -t   # one long line

Media fit — diagrams shrink, not scroll

A fixed-size SVG inside .media-fit caps at its container instead of forcing sideways scroll.

Inspector split — reveals a third pane ≥ 1200px

.split-pane--inspector stacks below 800, is master/detail 800–1200, and reveals the inspector pane at --bp-wide. All tracks minmax(0,1fr) — overflow-safe.
