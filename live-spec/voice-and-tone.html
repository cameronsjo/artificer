Foundations · Voice & Tone

how artificer talks.

Literal. Direct. Lightly deadpan. Microcopy is a load-bearing wall — it tells the user what just happened, what to do next, and where they are. Anything else is decoration.

Three pillars

Literal

Name the thing. Don't gesture at it. "Build failed" not "Things went sideways."

Direct

Front-load the verb. "Save changes" not "Click here to save your changes."

Deadpan

Wit lives in restraint, not jokes. A list of exactly 5 things is funnier than a pun.

Empty states

Empty isn't a failure — it's a starting point. Tell the user what this surface is for and the single next action.

Generic / cute

Looks like there's nothing here yet!

Why not start by adding your first project?

Names the surface, gives one action

No projects yet.

Projects group runs, files, and notes by topic.

→ New project

Pep talk

Your inbox is all clear. Time to tackle something else!

Confirms state, exits

Inbox empty.

New items arrive when an agent flags one for review.

Errors

An error message has three jobs: what broke, why, how to fix. Skip any of the three and the message is incomplete.

Vague + apologetic

Oops! Something went wrong saving your work. Please try again.

Specific + actionable

Save failed. Network unreachable.

Your draft is kept locally. Retry when back online.

Metaphor

Whoops, the gremlins ate your input. Mind giving it another shot?

Names the field, names the rule

API key invalid.

Missing sk- prefix. Paste the full key from the console.

Stack trace dump

TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'map') at Object.<anonymous> (/build/index.js:42:18)…

Translated for a human, link to the rest

Success

Confirm the thing happened, then get out of the way. No celebration.

Excessive

Awesome! Your changes have been successfully saved to the cloud!

Confirms, dismisses

Saved. 2 minutes ago.

Vague past tense

Your settings have been updated.

Names what changed

Theme set to Light. Reverts on browser close.

Loading

If a wait is < 1s, say nothing. If 1–10s, name the step. If > 10s, name the step and the rough duration.

Generic spinner copy

Loading…

Please wait while we work our magic.

Names the step

Indexing 1,247 files.

~30 seconds. Cancel

Buttons & CTAs

Verb-first, two words preferred. Avoid noun-only labels — they read as taxonomy, not action.

Generic

[ OK ] · [ Submit ] · [ Click here ]

Specific verb + object

[ Save changes ] · [ Send invite ] · [ Open log ]

Question for a label

[ Are you sure? ] · [ Want to delete? ]

Decisive verb pair

[ Delete project ] / [ Keep ]

Confirmations

For destructive or irreversible actions, the dialog must answer what will happen and what won't.

Vague

Delete this?

This action cannot be undone.

Names the object & the consequence

Delete project "Atlas"?

12 runs and 47 files will be removed. Notes survive in your archive.

Glossary — words to swap

A standing list of words that drift toward apology, hedge, or marketing. Use the right column.

ContextAvoidUse
Errors Oops! (nothing — name the failure)
Errors Something went wrong X failed: [reason]
Permission Sorry, you can't do that Requires admin access
Loading Hang tight… Indexing… ~30s
Empty Nothing here yet! No [things] yet.
Saved Awesome! Saved! Saved · 2 min ago
Marketing Powerful · Seamless · Magical (none — describe what it does)
Hedging You might want to… Try [action]. or just: [action]
Anthropom. I'm thinking… Reasoning… or Searching files…
CTA Submit · Click here Send · Save · Open
Confirm Are you sure? Delete X? — [Delete] [Keep]

The 7-point checklist

  1. 01
    Name the surface or object. "No projects yet" beats "Nothing here."
  2. 02
    Front-load the verb. The first word of a button is what it does.
  3. 03
    Three jobs for an error: what broke · why · how to fix.
  4. 04
    No emoji in product copy. (Wordmarks and avatars are fine.)
  5. 05
    No metaphor in failures. "Gremlins" / "magic" / "sideways" are confusing under stress.
  6. 06
    Don't celebrate. A success message is a receipt, not a parade.
  7. 07
    Read it back at 1.5× speed. If anything feels like filler, cut it.
