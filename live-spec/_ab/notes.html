Verdict — resolved

Cameron, 2026-06-06: canonical .note ships with no font-family — it inherits the body face. That makes it mono on tool surfaces and sans on document surfaces, so each surface's character carries through. Rationale: he reads enough monospace that the usual "mono is hard to skim" objection doesn't apply; the surface should own the family decision in one place (the body), not get re-litigated per utility.
