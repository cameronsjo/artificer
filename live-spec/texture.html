Page-grain — optional substrate

Faint by default

A whole-page substrate — put .tex-paper on a page wrapper and a speckle sits under everything and scrolls with the page. Two levels only: Faint (here) and .tex-paper--whisper. Capped there by intent — louder grain reads as dirt on a clean page.

Whisper

.tex-paper--whisper — the ceiling. The flowing wordmark is a hero's texture; this is the one whole-page exception.
